How we work

A holding company, and what that does not mean

G. VERNIERS is a company incorporated under Belgian law, registered with the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises under enterprise number 0898.652.936, based at De Meulenaerstraat in 9100 Sint-Niklaas and active since June 2008. Its registered activity at the seat is that of a holding company, and its establishment carries holding management activity: involvement in day-to-day management and representation of companies on the basis of the shareholdings held.

The word holding reliably attracts approaches it should not, so the boundary is worth stating first. We hold participations with our own capital. We do not manage money belonging to anyone else, do not provide investment services or advice, do not place financial instruments and do not solicit funds from the public — all of which would require authorisation from the FSMA that we neither hold nor claim. Nothing on this site is an offer of securities or an invitation to invest. Second, where we charge management fees to a company we hold, those fees correspond to services actually rendered by identifiable people at a price that can be substantiated; fees without substance are disallowed, and the adjustment falls on the paying company as well as on us. And where we accept a directorship, Belgian law requires us to designate a permanent representative, a named individual carrying the same liability as if they held the mandate personally.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can you manage my investments or take in my money?

No. We hold participations on our own account with our own capital. Managing assets for others, giving investment advice, placing financial instruments or taking in funds from the public all require authorisation from the FSMA, which we do not hold and do not claim. If that is what you are looking for, you need an authorised firm, and we would rather say so immediately than have the conversation continue.

Is this an invitation to invest alongside you?

No. Nothing on this site is an offer of securities, a financial promotion or an invitation to invest, and it should not be read as one. We are describing what the company does because it has a public register entry and a name that invites assumptions. There is no fund, no external mandate and no pooled capital.

Why do you make a point about management fees?

Because fees charged between related companies have to correspond to real services at a defensible price, and Belgian tax authorities regularly disallow those that do not. When that happens the adjustment falls on the company that paid as well as the one that invoiced, so a round quarterly figure with nothing behind it is a shared problem rather than a tidy arrangement. We document what was done, by whom, and how the fee was set.

What is a permanent representative?

Where a company holds a directorship in another company, the Code of Companies and Associations requires it to designate a permanent representative: a natural person who incurs the same civil and criminal liability as if they exercised the mandate in their own name. It exists so that a human being is accountable for a board seat rather than only a legal entity. We designate one for any mandate we accept.

Do you handle the accounts and tax for the companies you hold?

No. Keeping books, preparing annual accounts and giving tax advice are reserved in Belgium to professionals registered with the ITAA, statutory audit to registered auditors, and legal advice to the Bar. We provide management and governance support and we refer those matters out. A holding offering to do all of it in-house would be crossing lines that exist for a reason.

What return can a participation be expected to produce?

None that we would state. Returns, distributions and valuations depend on how the companies concerned actually perform and on market conditions nobody controls. Anyone describing a holding's historic figures as an indication of what is achievable is making a claim rather than a statement of fact.
